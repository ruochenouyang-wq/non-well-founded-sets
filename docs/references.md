# References and Scope of the Literature Comparison

The repository does not claim mathematical priority. These references locate related ideas; theorems from the literature are not inserted as unverified Lean axioms.

- Peter Aczel, *Non-Well-Founded Sets* (1988). A central reference for anti-foundation. The project's comparison with the complete book remains unfinished.
- Marco Forti and Furio Honsell, *Set theory with free construction principles* (1983). Relevant to existence, extension and uniqueness principles. [Article](https://www.numdam.org/item/ASNSP_1983_4_10_3_493_0.pdf). Exact historical inclusion comparisons remain open in this project.
- Ali Sadegh Daghighi, Mohammad Golshani, Joel David Hamkins and Emil Jerabek, *The foundation axiom and elementary self-embeddings of the universe*. Relevant to BAFA, Quine atoms, automorphisms and the connection with permutation models. Its choice and global-choice assumptions must be tracked when comparing statements. [Author-hosted text](https://users.math.cas.cz/~jerabek/papers/dghj.pdf).
- J. J. M. Rutten, *Universal coalgebra: a theory of systems*, CWI report CS-R9652 (1996). Context for transition systems, quotient maps and factorization. [Report](https://ir.cwi.nl/pub/4802/4802D.pdf).
- Yde Venema, *Lectures on the modal mu-calculus*, 2024 notes. Context for modal invariance and the hypotheses of converse results. [Notes](https://tsinghualogic.net/JRC/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/tsinghua-notes-vs2.pdf).
- Martin Otto, *Graded modal logic and counting bisimulation*. A neighboring direction for counting-sensitive observations. Only metadata and the abstract were used in the earlier comparison. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1910.00039v2).
- Philipp Kleppmann, *Free Groups and the Axiom of Choice*, Cambridge doctoral thesis (2015), Section 1.5. Terminology for choice for n-element sets. This repository explicitly uses an internal set-of-sets formulation of pair choice. [Thesis](https://www.repository.cam.ac.uk/bitstream/1810/253759/1/thesis.pdf).
- Paul Howard and Jean E. Rubin, *Consequences of the Axiom of Choice*. Metadata was checked, but no exact entry number was verified for the candidate used here. [Publisher identifier](https://doi.org/10.1090/surv/059).
- Daheng Ju, *A new kind of anti-foundation axioms* (2026 preprint). A relevant comparison for proposals based on graph relations; a detailed comparison with this project's assumptions remains unfinished. [Preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2608.21682v1).

These entries summarize the existing project literature review. This publication-content review did not constitute a new exhaustive literature search. A successful Lean build verifies the encoded implications, not their novelty.
